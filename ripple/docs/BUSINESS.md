# Business summary

**Product:** AI-assisted change-impact intelligence for pull requests. **Model:** B2B SaaS subscription.
**First customers:** software startups with 5–50 developers; buyer is CTO / head of engineering.

| Plan | Price | Enforced limits (`service.PLAN_LIMITS`) |
|---|---|---|
| Free | ₹0 | 1 repo, 20 analyses/month |
| Developer Pro | ₹799/month | 10 repos, 500 analyses/month |
| Team | ₹4,999/month | 50 repos, 5,000 analyses/month |
| Enterprise | Custom | Unlimited, audit logs, private deployment |

Competitors: Sourcegraph, Semgrep, CodeScene, Launchable, Datadog, Softagram. Differentiator to *prove*: evidence-backed
PR-level impact combining structure, tests and (later) runtime and API contracts.
Market statistics quoted in the original blueprint support the problem's existence; they do not prove demand for this feature set.
